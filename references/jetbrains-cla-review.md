# JetBrains 1.3: findings and questions for legal review

Reviewed 17 October 2026. D234 leaves substantive contributor-agreement modifications and drafting/review to a qualified lawyer. This document records source findings and questions, not proposed contract language or an adoption-ready adaptation. No contributor agreement has been selected.

## Business brief

- Standard Elastic License 2.0 remains selected for Clacks's own application code under D221.
- Individual personal self-hosting is allowed; hosting for others, whether paid or free, is reserved under D219/D220.
- Accepted contributions need an appropriate rights arrangement for the intended hosted offering.
- Neither requiring nor excluding continuing-original-license availability is an accepted requirement. Future unrestricted relicensing is not an accepted requirement either.
- TrustedQSL-derived work, dependencies and geographic data retain separately applicable upstream rights and obligations.

## Source findings

The [Apache individual agreement](https://www.apache.org/licenses/icla.pdf), sections 4–8, and [Harmony license agreement](https://www.harmonyagreements.org/docs/ha-cla-i-v1.pdf), sections 3–6, provide comparison material. They are not selected replacements.

## Questions for counsel

1. Which existing agreement, if any, fits the hosting and public-licensing requirements with an acceptable amount of adaptation? Is reuse of its text appropriate?
2. What assurances should contributors give, and what liability follows? In particular, assess future-facing promises, unknown rights, ordinary defects/maintenance and later changes outside a contributor's control.
3. How should contributor-owned modifications and separately licensed upstream material be distinguished, including translated/adapted reference code?
4. What acceptance process, prior-contribution coverage and version records are appropriate? How should support material and intentional contributions be distinguished?
5. Who receives the rights, what project scope applies, and how should organizational authority, minors, personal information and record retention be handled?
6. What governing law, forum, transfer/successor, disclaimer and liability provisions are appropriate?
7. What policy choice, if any, should be made about continued original-license availability versus additional licensing discretion? Neither outcome should be inferred from the hosting requirement alone.

The named legal recipient, jurisdiction, final agreement and contribution-acceptance process remain unselected. No signing workflow has been deployed.
